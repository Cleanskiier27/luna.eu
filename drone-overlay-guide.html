Drone overlay and recursion guide

View NASA Worldview in the player to explore dated satellite imagery without sharing your screen.

Open the simulated overlay. The camera panels are generated graphics, not footage from a drone.

Preview without a hall-of-mirrors effect

Keep the overlay in one tab. In a separate tab, open the live session player, click Choose session, and select the overlay tab.

If you see smaller copies of the player inside itself, the capture includes its own output. Click Stop sharing and select only the overlay tab, not the player or the whole screen.

If the browser blocks capture from a local file, run npm start in E:\Luna-site and open the localhost player.
